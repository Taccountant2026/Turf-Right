Using this website

A clear starting point.

This website is operated by Leighton Hepworth for Stone & Sprig. Updated 20 October 2026.

Garden ideas and planning tools

The journal and planner provide general garden information. They cannot assess your particular site, plant, weather, tools or project. Use suitable instructions and plant-specific guidance, and ask for appropriate professional advice where a job needs it.

The planned shop

Catalogue products, prices, pictures and delivery calculations are provisional. All items are coming soon, stock is unconfirmed and checkout is closed. Saving a basket is not an order, reservation or contract. Confirmed sales information and terms must be published before orders open.

Project enquiries

Submitting a brief or question asks us to discuss it. It does not confirm coverage, availability, a visit or work. Scope, practical details, price, timing and any payment arrangements must be agreed in writing before proceeding.

Your messages

Share only information you have the right to provide. Keep passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information and private details about others out of enquiries. Messages are private and do not become public community posts.

Pictures and garden notes

The home-page garden picture is AI-generated inspiration, not a completed customer project. Product artwork is illustrative and does not show a confirmed supplied model. External resources have their own content and terms.

Questions

Use the contact form to ask Leighton about the site, a project or a planned product. Garden phone, public email, sales details and service coverage remain to be confirmed.
