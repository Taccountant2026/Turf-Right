Seasonal notes

An autumn reset, without clearing everything

Start with the paths you use, the lawn beneath your feet and a little space for wildlife.

Stone & Sprig journal · 3 min read · Updated 20 October 2026

Choose one useful job

Clear fallen leaves from the lawn and well-used paths. Work in small sections, with manageable loads rather than an overfilled bag.

Leave some life standing

Seed heads and some perennial stems can remain through winter as habitat. A tidy path and a less tidy border can sit happily together.

Let conditions decide

Avoid working on a frosty or waterlogged lawn. Check plant-specific advice before cutting back; autumn is not the right pruning season for every plant.
