Orders & returns

Questions before
the shop opens.

Stone & Sprig is not currently taking online orders or payments.

No order is created by saving a basket

A saved basket is a personal planning list on this device. It has no order number, payment or reserved stock, and there is nothing to cancel or return through that list.

Sales policies are being prepared

Confirmed seller details, delivery arrangements and the applicable order, cancellation, return and faulty-goods information will be available before products can be purchased. No paid return service or shortened return period is being offered here.

Already have a question?

If you believe you have paid or agreed work elsewhere, contact Leighton with the reference and where that agreement was made. Do not include card numbers, passwords or bank details. We can review the circumstances; this page does not change any rights or agreement you already have.
