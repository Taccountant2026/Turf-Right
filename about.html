Our approach

Good gardens.
Everyday beginnings.

Stone & Sprig is being built around a simple idea: a garden should be a space you can enjoy, with manageable steps towards making it your own.

A note from Leighton

I am building Stone & Sprig as a home for practical garden ideas, useful essentials and conversations about individual garden projects.

You can browse the planned shop, use the free planner and prepare a project brief today. The shop is still being prepared: stock, final product details and online payments must be confirmed before orders open.

Useful before impressive

We begin with what you need, what is already working and the time you actually have. A small, well-chosen job can be a good place to start.

Room for nature

A garden does not have to be spotless to be cared for. Our journal encourages thoughtful planting, sensible maintenance and space for wildlife.

Clear conversations

Every project is discussed first. We check scope, location, practical access, timing and price before any work is agreed.
